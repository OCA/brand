To apply pricelist items on branded products:

1.  See 'product_brand' module to learn how to configure brands on
    products.
2.  Go to *Sales \> Products \> Pricelists* and open the pricelist of your choice or
    create a new one.
3.  Edit the pricelist and add a new item to it.
4.  Select the product brand of your choice in the field *Brand*, leaving
    the category and product fields empty.
5.  Configure the rest of the rule the way you want, this rule will
    apply on any product from the brand you selected.
